DESIGNED AS A SOUND-TO-SPACE STUDY

Sound becomes space.

In Rainbows — A Galaxy of Sound is an interactive artefact inspired by listening to Radiohead's album. Each track becomes a different celestial environment. Its colors, rhythm, light, and movement are subjective translations of the listening experience, not claims about the artists' intentions.

The project extends Translating Sound from shapes and colors into an explorable world: an imagined galaxy of nostalgia, retro-futurism, floating, travel, and memory.

Choose one of the ten tracks to enter its visual world; adjust motion and light or remix the particles. A Spotify player is available for each track. For live sound-reactive visuals, start music and explicitly share browser tab audio or use the microphone; Spotify iframe audio cannot be read directly. Audio stays local in your browser.
